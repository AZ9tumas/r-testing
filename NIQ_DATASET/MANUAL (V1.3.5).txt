PREFACE

This dataset "THE NIQ-DATASET (V1.3.5)" based on a revision of working notes from Prof. em. Richard Lynn used for IQs of nations in Lynn and Vanhanen (2002, 2012). 
The main objectives of this revision are to verify the original data, achieve greater transparency, make methods and calculations more comprehensible and standardized.
It is also intended to continue the collection of data and keep methods and calculations up to date according to the latest state of research.
Tables were constructed in order to offer users the widest possible variety of variables for their individual purposes and trust in the collected data.
____________________________________________________________________________________________________

RECOMMENDATIONS FOR USE

For users who want quick access to IQ lists the table "FAV" (favorites) is recommended. 
It contains the final estimated national IQ-scores (NIQ) and descriptive statistics at the bottom but without additional information.
Ten variables for mean national IQ-scores are given here:

NIQ-UW (col. C):		Simple mean NIQs calculated from all samples for each nation included in THE NIQ-DATASET (V1.3)

NIQ-NW (col. D):		Mean NIQs calculated from all samples for each nation included in THE NIQ-DATASET (V1.3) weighted by N of individuals of each sample

NIQ-QNW (col. E):		Mean NIQs calculated from all samples for each nation included in THE NIQ-DATASET (V1.3) weighted by dataquality and N of individuals of each sample

NIQ-SAS (col. F):		Mean NIQs calculated from school assessment studies (PISA; TIMSS; PIRLS) included in THE NIQ-DATASET (V1.3)

NIQ-QNF+SAS (col. G):		Means of NIQ-QNW (col. E) and NIQ-SAS (col. F)

NIQ-QNF+SAS+GEO (col. H):	Means of NIQ-QNW (col. E) and NIQ-SAS (col. F), and supplemented for missing nations by geographic means of neighboring countries

NIQ-L&V02 (col. I):		National IQ-scores from Lynn & Vanhanen (2002) estimated from psychometric tests only

NIQ-L&V02+GEO (col. J):		National IQ-scores from Lynn & Vanhanen (2002) supplemented for missing nations by geographic means of neighboring countries

NIQ-L&V12 (col. K):		National IQ-scores from Lynn & Vanhanen (2012) estimated from psychometric tests only

NIQ-L&V12+GEO (col. L):		National IQ-scores from Lynn & Vanhanen (2012) supplemented for missing nations by geographic means of neighboring countries

IQ(L&V12)-NIQ:			Differences between NIQ-QNF+SAS+GEO (col. H) and NIQ-L&V12+GEO (col. L)

NIQ-R (col. N):			National IQ-scores from Prof. Dr. Heiner Rindermann, calculated from L&V02 and school assessment studies (PISA; TIMSS; PIRLS)
				- See "Appendix I Data" in Becker & Rindermann (2016).

NIQ (QNW) older versions:	Mean NIQs calculated from all samples for each nation included in the earlier versions of the THE NIQ-DATASET weighted by dataquality and N of individuals of each sample

The table "NAT" (national) contains a more differenciated view on national IQ-scores including separated IQ-scores by type of test.

The table "REC" (records) contains data and background material at the sample-level used to calculate numbers in "NAT".

For all questions, uncertainties, comments, criticism, recommendations etc., please contact the corresponding author.
